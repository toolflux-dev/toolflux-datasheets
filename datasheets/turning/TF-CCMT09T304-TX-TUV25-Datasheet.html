Wear-resistant grade: for steady, continuous, lighter cuts. Turning grade for steel, stainless steel and cast iron, supplied on sharp finishing geometries (corner radius 0.4 mm and below) for light depths of cut and good surface finish. For stainless steel, step up to the stainless specialists TFX-TMC20 (CVD, continuous cuts) or TFX-TMV30 (PVD, interrupted cuts); for cast iron, to TFX-TKC20 (CVD) or TFX-TKV15 (PVD).

Setup & First Cut
First cutMid-range Vc · mid feed · ap in the middle of its range
Interrupted / scaled skinDrop Vc about 20% · keep feed steady · cut below the skin
Tool overhang≤ 1.5 × shank height
Finish passFeed at the lower bound · feed ≤ RE/2 for best finish
CoolantFlood helps chip control and size hold
Troubleshooting
Rapid flank wearReduce Vc 15–20%
Built-up edgeRaise Vc, or raise feed slightly
Edge chippingLower feed · shorten overhang · check clamping
Poor finishRaise Vc · feed ≤ RE/2 · check corner wear
Long chipsRaise feed · keep ap high enough for the chipbreaker to work
Vibration? Overhang is the biggest cause. Halving overhang stiffens the setup about 8× — fix that before touching cutting data.
