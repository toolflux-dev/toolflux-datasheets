Grade TFX-TMC25 — Coating Architecture
TFX-TMC25CVDM15–M30
CVD multilayer on carbide optimised for high-speed continuous stainless steel turning. Allows significantly higher cutting velocities than PVD grades in stable, uninterrupted stainless cuts. Best for production turning of austenitic and duplex stainless where cycle time is critical. For intermittent or low-speed stainless work, prefer TFX-TMV25 (PVD).
⬡
PVD AlTiN
High-Al AlTiN coating hardness up to 1300°C. Handles work-hardening zones in duplex and super-duplex stainless at low Vc / high fn.
◈
Tough WC-Co
Hardness ≈ 1500 HV. ISO M30–M40. High Co for impact resistance under the elevated cutting forces of duplex stainless.
