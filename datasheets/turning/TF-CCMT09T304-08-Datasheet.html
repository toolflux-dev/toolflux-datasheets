Grade TF-P15 — Coating Architecture
⬡
CVD Multilayer — Ti(C,N) + Al₂O₃ + TiN
Three-layer Chemical Vapour Deposition. TiCN base provides substrate adhesion and hardness. Alumina mid-layer is a thermal barrier that resists cratering at high speeds. TiN outer layer serves as a gold-coloured wear indicator — edge is worn when gold disappears.
◈
Fine-Grain WC-Co Substrate
Tungsten Carbide with Cobalt binder, fine-grain structure. Hardness ≈ 1580–1640 HV. Equivalent ISO range: P10–P25. Balanced wear resistance and toughness for stable continuous turning.
◉
Gold TiN Wear Indicator
Outer TiN layer is gold-coloured when new. As the cutting edge wears, the gold colour disappears at the contact zone — operator can visually detect worn edges instantly without measurement tools.
