Setup & First Cut
Centre height± 0.1 mm — most grooving failures start here
OverhangAbsolute minimum — grooving is vibration-sensitive, more so in hardened material
First cutLow-mid Vc · fn at lower bound
Wide groovesMultiple plunges, ≤ 0.6 × insert width step-over
CoolantMQL or dry — flood coolant risks thermal shock on the coating at these hardness levels
Why start here: hardened steel work-hardens further if the edge rubs instead of cuts — rigidity and a conservative first pass matter more than chasing top Vc.
Troubleshooting
Chipping at the edgeLower fn · check for interrupted/scaled surface
Insert breakageCheck centre height ± 0.1 mm · reduce Vc and fn together
Rapid flank wearConfirm hardness ≤ 55 HRC · lower Vc 10–15%
Poor floor finishLower fn on final pass · avoid dwell at depth
Burning / discolorationSwitch to MQL or dry · reduce Vc
Vibration? Biggest cause is overhang. Halving overhang stiffens the setup 8× (stiffness ∝ 1/L³) — fix that before touching cutting data, especially above 45 HRC.
