3× checked = confirmed by the insert maker’s data, its printed catalogue and an independent maker’s same-class grade. 2× checked = maker data and printed catalogue agree. Conservative = the printed range is the safer (slower) side of what the references allow. Start in the lower half of each range until chip form and edge wear settle.
Wear-resistant grade: feed and depth of cut are set lighter than the tougher TFX grade on the same insert.

Troubleshooting
Chip jammingRaise feed · check coolant reaches the edge
VibrationShorten overhang · check centre height
Edge chippingLower feed · check the insert is fully seated
Pip left at centreCheck centre height · slow feed near centre
Vibration? Overhang is the biggest cause. Halving overhang stiffens the setup about 8× — fix that before touching cutting data.
