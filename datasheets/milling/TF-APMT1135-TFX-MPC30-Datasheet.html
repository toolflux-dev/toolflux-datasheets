Grade TFX-MPC30 — Coating Architecture
⬡
PVD TiAlN + TiN
Physical Vapour Deposition TiAlN base layer with TiN outer layer. The TiAlN provides strong oxidation resistance up to 850°C and handles abrasive wear in steel and stainless cutting. The TiN outer layer adds surface hardness and acts as a wear indicator — the gold colour fades at the contact zone when the edge approaches its wear limit.
◈
Fine-Grain WC-Co Substrate
Fine-grain Tungsten Carbide with Cobalt binder. Hardness ≈ 1580 HV. ISO P10–P30 / M10–M25. Slightly higher Co content than TFX-MP25 for better micro-chip resistance in variable-engagement shoulder milling. Optimised for steel and austenitic stainless without superalloy range.
◉
TiN Wear Indicator
The gold TiN outer layer disappears at the cutting contact zone as the edge wears — edge condition is visible without a microscope or measuring tool. In high-production environments, this lets you schedule insert changes by inspection rather than by fixed cycle count, reducing both premature changes and surprise failures.

Check screw seating before each roughing run — high chip loads generate lateral forces that can back out undertightened screws. Replace any screw showing thread wear.
Application Guide
  • ⬛
    Face & Shoulder Milling — Steel and Stainless Primary application. TFX-MPC30 is optimised for P10–P30 and M10–M25. Vc 140–220 m/min on alloy steel, 110–180 m/min on stainless 304/316. Use when TFX-MP25 shows accelerated flank wear on harder alloy steels.
  • 🥇
    TiN Wear Indicator — Production Use The gold outer TiN layer fades at the cutting contact zone as the edge wears. Inspect the insert under lighting after each cycle — a clearly worn gold zone signals time to index. Eliminates guesswork on tool change intervals.
  • 📐
    Step Shoulder — Carbon and Alloy Steel ap up to 2.5 mm, ae up to 8 mm. The slightly higher Co substrate content handles micro-chipping at shoulder entry better than pure finishing grades under variable engagement.
  • 🔄
    MP25 vs MP35 — When to Choose Use TFX-MP25 when machining includes superalloys (S range) or when you need the absolute sharpest edge for thin-wall stainless. Use TFX-MPC30 when the material is steel or stainless only and you want the TiN wear indicator for production scheduling.
Stainless tip: Keep fz ≥ 0.05 mm/tooth minimum — dwelling below this builds up a work-hardened layer that destroys the next pass. Flood coolant mandatory on 304/316.
