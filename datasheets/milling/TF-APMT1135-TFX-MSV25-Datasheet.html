Setup & First Cut
Milling directionClimb (down) milling preferred
Runout at insert≤ 0.02 mm — check before cutting
First cutMid-range Vc · mid fz · ap ≤ half of max
EntryRoll into cut where possible — avoid straight plunge
CoolantDry or air blast preferred for carbide milling
Why start here: interrupted cutting punishes carbide through thermal cycling — dry cutting with moderate parameters usually outlives flooded aggressive ones.
Troubleshooting
Rapid flank wearReduce Vc 15–20%
Edge chippingLower fz · check runout ≤ 0.02 mm · check seat
Built-up edgeRaise Vc or fz · avoid re-cutting chips
Poor finishCheck runout first · then lower fz
VibrationFewer teeth in cut · lower ap · shorten overhang
Vibration? Biggest cause is overhang. Halving overhang stiffens the setup 8× (stiffness ∝ 1/L³) — fix that before touching cutting data.
