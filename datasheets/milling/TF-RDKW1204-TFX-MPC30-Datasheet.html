Grade TFX-MPC30 — Coating Architecture
⬡
CVD MT-TiCN+Al₂O₃ + TiN
Physical Vapour Deposition MT-TiCN wear layer under an Al₂O₃ thermal barrier with TiN top coat. The CVD stack handles thermal load and abrasion. The TiN outer layer adds surface hardness and provides a visible wear indicator — the gold colour fades when the edge approaches wear limit.
◈
Fine-Grain WC-Co Substrate
Fine-grain WC-Co, hardness ≈ 1580 HV. ISO P10–P30 / M10–M25. Steel and stainless focused. Slightly higher Co content vs TFX-MP25 for better resistance to micro-chipping in variable-engagement cuts.
◉
TiN Wear Indicator
Gold TiN outer layer disappears at the contact zone as the edge wears — operator can judge edge condition visually without a microscope. Useful in high-production environments for scheduling tool changes.

🔩
Clamping Screw — Torx T20 · M4 × 8 mm (countersunk taper head)
RDKW holders use a countersunk taper-head screw (taper 40–60°) through the 4.4mm hole. Thread: M4. Length: 8mm. Drive: Torx T20. Tightening torque: 2.5–3.0 Nm. The taper head self-centres the round insert in the pocket — critical for runout control. Never reuse a screw with worn taper; insert will rock under cutting load.
Application Guide
  • 🔁
    Copy Milling & 3D ContouringFull round geometry allows curved toolpaths, gentle entry angles, and step-over patterns that flat inserts cannot track. Ideal for die and mould cavities, cam profiles, and sculptured surfaces.
  • 📉
    Ramping & Helical EntryRamp angle up to 6° (vs 2° for APMT series). Helical interpolation entry preferred over plunge — distributes entry load across the full round cutting radius.
  • 🔲
    Step-Down FinishingUse ap = 0.5–2.0mm per pass with small ae for finishing. The round geometry produces a smooth scallop with low Ra. Match step-over to required surface finish and tool diameter.
  • 🔄
    8-Position IndexingRotate the insert to next position when edge shows wear at the contact zone. Mark indexed positions on the box to track usage. All 8 positions must be used before discarding — cost per edge is 1/8 of insert price.
Runout check before use: Seat the insert, torque the screw, then verify spindle runout ≤ 0.01mm at the cutting radius with a dial gauge. Round inserts are more sensitive to runout than parallelogram inserts — even 0.02mm runout doubles the effective chip load on one side.
