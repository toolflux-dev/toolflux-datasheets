Grade TFX-MPC30 — Coating Architecture
⬡
PVD TiAlN + TiN
Physical Vapour Deposition TiAlN base layer with TiN outer layer. The TiAlN base handles thermal load and abrasion. The TiN outer layer adds surface hardness and provides a visible wear indicator — the gold colour fades when the edge approaches wear limit.
◈
Fine-Grain WC-Co Substrate
Fine-grain WC-Co, hardness ≈ 1580 HV. ISO P10–P30 / M10–M25. Steel and stainless focused. Slightly higher Co content vs TFX-MP25 for better resistance to micro-chipping in variable-engagement cuts.
◉
TiN Wear Indicator
Gold TiN outer layer disappears at the contact zone as the edge wears — operator can judge edge condition visually without a microscope. Useful in high-production environments for scheduling tool changes.

Application Guide
  • ⬛
    Face Milling — Large SurfacesAPMT1604 covers more surface per pass than APMT1135 (LE 14mm vs 9mm). Run in Ø32mm+ cutters at ae up to 75% cutter diameter. Vc 160–230 m/min on structural steel.
  • 📐
    Step Shoulder — 90° WallsKeep ap ≤ 3.0mm and ae ≤ 7mm for clean walls. Larger insert gives stronger edge than APMT1135 under lateral milling forces.
  • 🔄
    BAP300R vs BAP400RBAP300R = standard face/shoulder mill. BAP400R = heavy-duty — use for large DOC, forged surfaces, or when BAP300R vibrates. APMT1604 fits both series.
APMT1135 vs APMT1604: Use APMT1135 in Ø25mm holders for small parts and tight clearances. Use APMT1604 in Ø32mm+ holders where you need higher metal removal rate or are milling wide surfaces in one pass.
